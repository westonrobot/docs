# Systems

Pre-integrated combinations of compute, power, networking and sensing, supplied as one unit and mounted on a robot base. The components are the same ones documented under [Peripherals](/peripheral/intro) — the difference is that here they arrive wired, configured and tested together, so you start from a working platform rather than an assembly job.

<ProductGrid columns={3}>

<ProductCard to="/system/ugv_devkit" src={require('./ugv_devkit/img/overview.jpg').default} alt="UGV Development Kits mounted on robot bases" title="UGV Development Kit">Compute, power and networking on a robot base, for mapping, navigation and perception work. Covers both the v1.0 and v1.1 generations.</ProductCard>

</ProductGrid>

Not sure which generation you have? The onboard computer is the quickest tell — a [NanoPC](/peripheral/computer/nanopc) is v1.0, a [reComputer J4012](/peripheral/computer/j4012) is v1.1.
