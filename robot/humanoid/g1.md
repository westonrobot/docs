# G1

![Unitree G1 humanoid robot](../img/unitree/G1_robot.png)

The G1 is a humanoid robot that stands 130 cm tall and weighs 35 kg. It comes equipped with a LIVOX MID-360 LiDAR and an Intel RealSense D435i depth camera.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Height | 130 cm |
| Weight | 35 kg |
| LiDAR | LIVOX MID-360 |
| Depth camera | Intel RealSense D435i |
| Onboard computers | 2 (control unit + user development unit) |
| Development access | Wired only — [why](#does-the-robot-support-wireless-development) |
| Weather resistance | Not waterproof |

Degrees of freedom, payload, battery capacity and runtime are not recorded here yet. Until they are, the [Unitree G1 developer pages](https://support.unitree.com/home/en/G1_developer) and the [G1 features page](https://www.unitree.com/app/g1) are the authoritative sources.

## Identify your unit

The **serial number** is in the **battery compartment**. It is also shown, along with the model, in the mobile app under `Device → Robot Settings`.

Have it ready before contacting support — it tells us the exact hardware revision and what firmware shipped with it. See [Identify your product](/support/identify-your-product) for other platforms.

### Onboard computers

G1 Edu has two built-in computers:

| Unit | IP | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operation & control | `192.168.123.161` | Not accessible to the public |
| User development | `192.168.123.164` | Username `unitree`, password `123` |

Development requires a wired connection. The development unit can join a WiFi network for *internet access* only — see the [G1 Internet Connection Guide](/tutorial/unitree/g1_internet_guide).

## Troubleshooting & FAQ

### Can the robot stand on its own?

From a lying position, the G1 can stand on its own. However, this mode is not recommended as it may damage the joints from excessive use, and the robot may fall on smooth floors. It is recommended to have the robot stand from a seated or hanging position instead.

### Does the robot support wireless development?

The robot's internal development PC can connect to WiFi networks for internet access — see the [G1 Internet Connection Guide](/tutorial/unitree/g1_internet_guide) — but it does not support wireless development.
