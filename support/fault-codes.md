:::danger This page is a placeholder

**No codes are listed yet.** The structure is in place; the codes themselves have to come from firmware and vendor sources rather than be inferred, and a wrong entry here would send someone down the wrong diagnostic path.

Until this page is populated, read the fault via the mobile app or controller and follow the platform's diagnostics guide:
[G1](/tutorial/unitree/g1_diag_guide) · [Go2](/tutorial/unitree/go2_diag_guide) · [B2](/tutorial/unitree/b2_diag_guide)
